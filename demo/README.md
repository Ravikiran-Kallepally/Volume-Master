# Volume Master — DEMO (Premium Preview)

A sandbox copy of the extension for trying future and premium features.
**This folder never ships.** Store zips are built from the root files only.

## How to try it

1. Go to `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**
2. **Turn OFF the real Volume Master** first. Two copies on the same page
   would both boost the audio, which doubles the volume
3. Click **Load unpacked** and pick this `demo/` folder
4. The popup shows an orange **DEMO** badge, so you always know which one is open

When a feature proves itself here, port it to the root files for a real release.

## Ideas: features that bring people back

Goal: make Volume Master part of people's daily routine, but keep it simple
and enjoyable. The best habit features work **automatically**, so users feel
the extension every day without having to open it.

| # | Feature | Why people come back | Free / Premium |
|---|---------|----------------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Sound modes**: one tap for Movie, Voice (podcasts, lectures, calls), Music, Night | A quick reason to open the popup for each kind of audio | Premium |
| 2 | **Night mode / quiet hours**: after a set time, cap loud peaks and lift quiet dialogue | Runs every evening automatically | Premium |
| 3 | **Auto-level**: every tab plays at a similar loudness; no more blasting ads or whisper-quiet videos | Felt on every video, every day | Premium |
| 4 | **Auto-duck for calls**: when a call starts in one tab, other tabs quietly lower, then come back | Fits straight into the work day | Premium |
| 5 | **Solo this tab**: one click mutes every other tab | Fast, satisfying, used often | Free |
| 6 | **Control other tabs from the popup** (feedback backlog #1) | Fewer clicks, power-user delight | Free |
| 7 | **On-screen volume display for shortcuts** (feedback backlog #4) | Makes shortcuts feel polished | Free |
| 8 | **Equalizer**: bass / treble, with a bass boost preset | The classic upgrade people expect | Premium |

Keep the free tier generous; the survey results (v3.4) decide pricing and
which premium features to build first.
