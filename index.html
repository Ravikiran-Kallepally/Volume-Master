FAQ

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no ads, no account and no trial. There's no paywall in front of 1000% boost or per-site memory.

Why does it need access to all websites?

To boost a page's audio, the extension has to connect to that page's audio pipeline, and you might want to boost any site. It doesn't read the page's content, and it doesn't collect or send any data.

Will boosting damage my speakers or my hearing?

Smart Boost keeps loud peaks from crackling, which is easier on speakers. Very high levels can still be uncomfortably loud, especially on headphones, so turn it up gradually and take it easy on your ears.

How do I make a site always play at the same volume?

Set the level you like, then tap "Save for site". Next time you open that site, the level comes back by itself. Manage all saved sites from the list icon in the popup.

Does it work on games and embedded videos?

Yes. It works on video and audio players, players embedded in other pages, and browser games built with HTML5, WebGL, Unity or Flash (via Ruffle).

Can I control it with my keyboard?

Yes: Alt+Shift+↑ and Alt+Shift+↓ change the volume by 10%, and Alt+Shift+M mutes. You can change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Email ravikirankallepally@gmail.com with the site name. Most of our updates started as messages like that.
