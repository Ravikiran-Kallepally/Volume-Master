1. GETTING STARTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

After installing the extension, click the Volume Master Remastered icon in your
Chrome toolbar to open the control panel.

The panel shows:
  • The current tab's title and favicon
  • A large volume percentage display
  • The main volume slider (0% – 1000%)
  • Quick preset buttons
  • Smart Boost toggle
  • Mute, Save/Unsave, and Reset controls
  • A list of other tabs currently playing audio
  • A Share button in the header

To control a tab's volume, simply open the popup while that tab is active and
move the slider. Volume changes apply instantly.

Note: Volume Master Remastered cannot control audio on Chrome system pages
(chrome://...), the Chrome Web Store, or browser-level pages. It works on all
regular websites including streaming services and anime sites.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE VOLUME SLIDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Range: 0% to 1000%

The slider is color-coded to help you judge boost levels at a glance:

  Violet  (0%   – 100%)   Normal range — matches your system volume
  Green   (100% – 200%)   Mild boost — clean on most content
  Amber   (200% – 400%)   High boost — use Smart Boost for best results
  Red     (400% – 1000%)  Extreme boost — always use Smart Boost here

A small ⚡ marker on the slider shows the 400% threshold where Smart Boost
automatically activates.

Dragging left of the 100% mark reduces volume (useful for quieting a loud tab
without changing your system volume).

The large number in the centre always shows your exact current percentage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. QUICK PRESETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Five preset buttons let you jump to common levels instantly:

  [ 50%  ]  Quiet — half your system volume
  [ 100% ]  Normal — no boost or reduction
  [ 200% ]  2× boost — good for quiet videos and podcasts
  [ 400% ]  4× boost — for very quiet recordings
  [ 1000%]  10× boost — maximum, use with Smart Boost enabled

Clicking a preset also clears the mute state and triggers the Smart Boost
auto-mode check (same as dragging the slider).

4. SMART BOOST — CLEAN AUDIO AT HIGH VOLUMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Smart Boost is the most important feature for listening at high volumes.

  WHAT IT DOES
  ────────────
  At high gain levels (above ~300%), raw audio amplification causes clipping —
  a harsh, distorted sound that occurs when the signal exceeds the hardware's
  maximum output level.

  Smart Boost adds a DynamicsCompressor to the audio chain. It acts as a
  transparent limiter: it only engages near the peak level, catching loud
  spikes before they distort, while leaving quiet passages untouched. The
  result is louder perceived volume without the harshness.

  Think of it as the difference between turning an amplifier up until it
  clips versus using a professional limiter to push loudness cleanly.

  AUTO-MODE
  ─────────
  Smart Boost automatically enables when volume crosses 400% going up, and
  automatically disables when you drop back below 400%. A toast notification
  confirms when it auto-enables.

  MANUAL OVERRIDE
  ───────────────
  Click the "⚡ Smart Boost" row to override the automatic behaviour at any
  time. The subtitle changes to "Manually enabled/disabled · Reset to restore
  auto" to confirm the override is active. Hit [Reset] to go back to auto-mode.

  WHEN TO USE IT
  ──────────────
  Volume               Recommendation
  ─────────────────    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  0% – 200%            Optional — most content sounds fine without it
  200% – 400%          Recommended — prevents distortion on loud passages
  400% – 1000%         Always use Smart Boost for usable audio quality

  WHEN NOT TO USE IT
  ──────────────────
  If you are monitoring or recording audio and need the unprocessed signal,
  leave Smart Boost off. The compressor slightly changes the dynamic range,
  which is undesirable in professional monitoring contexts.

  Smart Boost state is saved alongside your volume when you use "Save for site"
  so it will restore automatically on your favourite sites.

5. MUTE TOGGLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The [Mute] button instantly silences the current tab without moving the slider.
Your volume setting is preserved — clicking [Unmute] restores it exactly.

The volume percentage display dims and shows "MUTED" when active.
The button turns red when muted.

Useful for: quickly silencing an auto-playing ad without losing your boost
setting, or temporarily quieting a tab during a call.

You can also mute via keyboard shortcut (see Section 7).

The popup display updates live when you use the mute shortcut — you do not
need to close and reopen it to see the change.

6. PER-SITE MEMORY  (Save / Unsave)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Volume Master Remastered can remember your preferred volume for each website
and restore it automatically every time you visit.

  HOW TO SAVE
  ───────────
  1. Set your preferred volume (and Smart Boost setting) for the site.
  2. Click [Save for site].
  3. A toast confirmation appears, the bookmark icon lights up in the header,
     and the button changes to [Unsave].

  HOW IT WORKS
  ────────────
  The next time you open that website in any tab, the extension will
  automatically apply your saved volume within a second of the page loading.
  No need to open the popup.

  HOW TO UNSAVE
  ─────────────
  When a saved setting is active, the [Save for site] button is replaced by
  a red [Unsave] button. Click it to remove the saved preference immediately.
  The site will return to normal behaviour on the next visit.

  EXAMPLES
  ────────
  • Save YouTube at 200% + Smart Boost on  → always boosted when you open YouTube
  • Save a news site at 60%                → auto-quiets that site's loud videos
  • Save a podcast app at 400% + Smart Boost → clear speech on quiet recordings

  NOTE ON PER-PAGE SAVING
  ────────────────────────
  Settings are saved per hostname (e.g. twitch.tv), not per individual page
  URL. All pages on the same site share one saved setting.

7. KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Control the active tab's volume without opening the popup:

  Alt + Shift + Up Arrow     Increase volume by 10%
  Alt + Shift + Down Arrow   Decrease volume by 10%
  Alt + Shift + M            Toggle mute on/off

These shortcuts work globally in Chrome — you do not need to click the
extension icon first.

If the popup is already open, the volume display updates live with each
keystroke — you do not need to close and reopen it.

  CHANGING SHORTCUTS
  ──────────────────
  1. Go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts in your browser.
  2. Find "Volume Master Remastered" in the list.
  3. Click the pencil icon next to any shortcut and press your preferred keys.

Note: Chrome reserves many key combinations. If your chosen shortcut conflicts,
Chrome will warn you.

8. AUDIO TABS LIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The bottom section of the popup lists every other tab currently playing audio
or with a custom volume setting.

Each row shows:
  • The tab's favicon
  • The tab's title (truncated if long)
  • A volume badge:
      – "150%"          Boosted (shown in purple)
      – "Muted"         Muted (shown in red)
      – No badge        Playing at 100% (normal)
  • An arrow indicating it is clickable

Clicking any row switches Chrome's focus to that tab and brings its window
to the front if it is in a different Chrome window, then closes the popup.

This makes it easy to jump between multiple audio sources — for example,
switching between a music tab and a video call — without hunting through
your open tabs.

9. SHARE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Share button in the popup header lets you share Volume Master Remastered
with friends or communities in one tap.

Clicking Share opens a panel with:
  • LinkedIn, Facebook, Reddit, X / Twitter, WhatsApp — each opens the

10. TIPS & BEST PRACTICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  GET THE MOST FROM HIGH BOOST LEVELS
  ────────────────────────────────────
  • Always enable Smart Boost above 300% for clean, distortion-free audio.
  • The effective maximum usable volume depends on the source recording quality:
      – Well-mastered music/streaming : ~150% – 200%
      – YouTube videos                : ~200% – 300%
      – Podcasts and voice content    : ~300% – 500%
      – Quiet user recordings         : ~500% – 1000%

  QUIET TABS
  ──────────
  Use Volume Master Remastered below 100% to selectively quieten a specific
  tab rather than lowering your system volume. This keeps other tabs at their
  normal level.

  MULTIPLE AUDIO TABS
  ───────────────────
  Each tab has its own independent volume setting. You can boost one tab while
  muting another simultaneously. Use the Audio Tabs list to manage them from
  a single popup.

  AUDIO THAT STARTS AFTER THE PAGE LOADS
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  The extension uses a MutationObserver to detect audio and video elements
  added dynamically (e.g., after clicking Play on a streaming site). If you
  set a volume before pressing Play, the boost is applied as soon as the
  element appears.

  ANIME AND STREAMING SITES WITH EMBEDDED PLAYERS
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  The extension injects into all page frames including cross-origin iframes,
  so embedded video players on anime sites (Crunchyroll, 9anime, etc.) are
  fully supported. Volume changes are relayed automatically.

  RESETTING
  ─────────
  The [Reset] button returns volume to 100%, clears the mute state, and
  restores Smart Boost to auto-mode. It does NOT clear a saved per-site
  preference — use the [Unsave] button to remove a saved setting.

11. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  VOLUME BOOST HAS NO EFFECT
  ───────────────────────────
  • Make sure you are on a regular webpage (http:// or https://).
  • Try refreshing the page and re-opening the popup.
  • Some sites use protected media (DRM) which may limit audio access.
  • The extension now supports cross-origin iframes, so most embedded players
    including anime streaming sites should work. If a site still doesn't work,
    try refreshing after installing.

  AUDIO SOUNDS DISTORTED AT HIGH VOLUMES
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  Enable Smart Boost. Above 300%, distortion is expected without it as the
  raw signal exceeds the hardware's clipping point. Smart Boost auto-enables
  at 400% but you can turn it on manually at any volume.

  "CANNOT CONTROL AUDIO ON THIS PAGE" MESSAGE
  ────────────────────────────────────────────
  This appears on pages where Chrome does not allow extensions to run scripts:
    • chrome:// pages (settings, extensions, etc.)
    • The Chrome Web Store
    • about:// pages
  This is a Chrome security restriction that cannot be bypassed.

  EXTENSION ICON SHOWS A BLUE RECTANGLE IN THE TAB
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  This is Chrome's built-in indicator that an extension is modifying audio
  on the page. It is expected and intentional — Chrome requires it to keep
  users informed. You cannot hide it; pressing F11 (Windows) or Ctrl+Cmd+F
  (Mac) for full-screen will minimise its visual impact.

  SAVED VOLUME NOT RESTORING
  ───────────────────────────
  • The saved volume only applies once the page finishes loading completely.
  • If the site uses a single-page app architecture (e.g., YouTube), navigate
    fully to the page — the boost applies after each navigation.
  • Check that the volume was saved correctly by opening the popup: the
    bookmark icon in the header should be visible (lit up) and the button
    should read [Unsave].

  CLICKING AN AUDIO TAB DOES NOTHING
  ────────────────────────────────────
  If the tab is in a different Chrome window, that window should come to the
  front automatically. If it does not, try clicking the tab row again after
  the popup closes.

  KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS NOT WORKING
  ──────────────────────────────
  • Visit chrome://extensions/shortcuts and confirm the shortcuts are set
    under "Volume Master Remastered".
  • Chrome may have a conflict with another extension or system shortcut.
  • Try reassigning to a different key combination.

  POPUP VOLUME DISPLAY NOT UPDATING WHILE USING SHORTCUTS
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  The popup polls for changes every 500ms. Allow up to half a second for the
  display to reflect a keyboard shortcut change.

12. PRIVACY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Volume Master Remastered does not collect, transmit, or sell any data.

  • All settings are stored locally in Chrome's storage (chrome.storage.local)
    on your own device.
  • Per-site volume preferences never leave your browser.
  • No analytics, tracking, or network requests of any kind.
  • The extension requires "Read and change all your data on websites you visit"
    because it must access the AudioContext of any page you want to control.
    It reads nothing from page content — it only connects to the audio pipeline.
