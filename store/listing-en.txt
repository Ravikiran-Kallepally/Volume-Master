Volume Booster & Sound Booster for Chrome. Increase volume up to 1000% with Smart Boost compressor, per-site memory, and per-tab control.

Volume Master is a volume booster that puts you in control of audio in Chrome, boosts any tab from silence to 1000%, independently of your system volume. The sound booster you need when videos are just too quiet.

Drag the slider, pick a preset (50 / 100 / 200 / 400 / 1000%), or use keyboard shortcuts. Each tab has its own level. Mute one tab without touching the others.

🔊 𝐒𝐦𝐚𝐫𝐭 𝐁𝐨𝐨𝐬𝐭 :
Above 400%, the volume booster automatically switches on a compressor that catches loud peaks before they distort. You get genuinely louder audio, not just harsher noise. Toggle it off anytime if you prefer the raw signal.

Save a volume for any website and it restores automatically every visit — no popup needed. Your Smart Boost preference saves alongside it.
